Paperback

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Paperback (on Google Play: "Paperback Wallpapers") changes your lock-screen and home-screen wallpapers from photos you pick.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics of its own and no tracking, and the app sends nothing about you or your own photos anywhere.

Network access

The app goes online for two things:

  • Unsplash. While you browse, the app loads Unsplash's photos from Unsplash (https://unsplash.com), which, like any website, sees your phone's internet (IP) address. A search sends your search words to Unsplash. Adding one of its photos tells Unsplash it was downloaded, as Unsplash requires. A followed topic asks Unsplash for that topic's photos now and then, and loads the photo shown, as with any Unsplash photo. The app doesn't store Unsplash photos: they are loaded from Unsplash when they are shown (as a wallpaper, in your lists or in the crop editor), and when the app prepares them for smart fit or Darker photos (a small version, to find the faces in it or measure its brightness; this can happen in the background, for example after you add photos or when Night checks the time of day), so Unsplash sees your phone's internet address then too. Like a browser, the phone may keep a temporary cached copy of a photo it loaded recently; Android can clear it at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes it. Unsplash handles this under its own privacy policy (https://unsplash.com/privacy).
  • Google Play. Buying Pro goes through Google Play. Google Play's billing code in the app may send Google its own diagnostic information about its connection to Google Play (for example when the app checks whether Pro is unlocked), under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Nothing about your own photos and folders leaves your phone, except in a backup you make yourself. Android's own automatic app backup is switched off for Paperback, so Android doesn't copy the app's data or its copies of your photos to your Google account either.

Smart fit

Smart fit (Pro) finds the faces in your photos on your phone, with Android's built-in face finder. It keeps only where in each photo the faces are, so it can frame the photo around them. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing about who is in a photo is kept.

Backups

A backup file is made only when you ask for one (Settings, Make a backup), and goes only where you save it. It holds your lists and settings, your crops, the folders you watch, the links to your Unsplash photos, where smart fit found faces, and the app's copies of your picked photos; the app sends it nowhere.

Your photos

When you pick photos from Google Photos or Google Drive, the app keeps its own copies of those photos, only on your phone (in the app's private storage), so it can show them as wallpapers. The copies are never uploaded or shared. When you remove a photo from your lists, its copy is deleted; uninstalling the app deletes all of them.

When you choose a folder on your phone to watch, the app may only read that folder (never change or delete anything in it), reads its photos straight from it without copying them, and keeps what it finds on your phone; stopping watching gives that access back.

Purchases

Paperback Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app only learns from Google Play whether Pro was bought; it never sees your payment details.

Children

Paperback is not made for children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something new with your information, this page is updated first, with a new date at the top.
